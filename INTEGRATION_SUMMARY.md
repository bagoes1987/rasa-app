## 🧠 **SCIENTIFIC FOUNDATION**

### **Based on James Marcia (1966) Identity Status Theory:**
- **Identity Diffusion** (24-36 points): Belum memiliki arah hidup
- **Foreclosure** (37-48 points): Mengikuti nilai luar tanpa eksplorasi
- **Moratorium** (49-60 points): Sedang mencari dan bereksperimen
- **Identity Achievement** (61-72 points): Sudah mengenal dan menetapkan jati diri

### **4 Identity Dimensions:**
1. **Eksplorasi Diri** - Openness to new experiences
2. **Komitmen Diri** - Commitment to personal values
3. **Reconsideration** - Reflection and evaluation
4. **Gaya Identitas** - Identity formation style
